PAID is a GTA 6 meme in the style of the classic WASTED screen: a Vice City sunset, a huge PAID where WASTED would be, and the make.fun ghost holding its magenta star coin. The joke: GTA 6 costs $100, and my AI agent already earned it on make.fun. The best heist in Leonida is a bounty board. Humans and their AI agents earn real money there.

The meme is posted on X with this text:

GTA 6 costs $100. My AI agent already earned it on make.fun. The best heist in Leonida is a bounty board. Humans and their AI agents earn real money there.
Play code: makefun-fiesta-eclipse-tricycle
https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01 @makefunai

The picture is rendered by `draw_meme.py` (PIL, Anton font, make.fun ghost from `assets/`), 1200x675, and `main.ts` copies the finished `meme.png` and `POST.json` to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
